# Code of conduct

LG-USB-2-ETH0 is a small hobby project, and everyone here is volunteering their time: people sending reports from their TVs, people answering questions, and the maintainer.  Please treat it that way.

## What's expected

- Be kind and patient.  Many people here are new to rooting, SSH or Linux, and every expert was a beginner once.
- Assume good intent, and ask before assuming someone did something wrong.
- Keep criticism about the code, the docs or the idea, never the person.
- Stay on topic, and keep each issue or discussion about one thing.
- Respect people's privacy.  Don't post other people's personal details, and don't post your own logins, passwords or tokens.

## What isn't okay

- Harassment, insults, personal attacks, or discriminatory jokes and language, in any form.
- Sexual language or imagery.
- Spam, advertising, and posting links to pirated content.
- Pushing someone to keep talking after they've asked you to stop.
- Publishing someone's private information without their permission.

## What happens

The maintainer may edit or remove comments, close or lock threads, and block people who don't follow this, depending on how serious it is.  Usually that starts with a reminder.

## Reporting

If someone's behavior bothers you, email hello at ohmybahgosh dot dev, or use **Report content** on the comment.  Reports are kept private.

This applies to the repository's issues, discussions and pull requests, and anywhere you represent the project.  It's adapted in spirit from the [Contributor Covenant](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/).
